The Origin

“Francis, reimagined. Nova, a star newly born. Together, a house built on the belief that every legend deserves a second first drive.”

✦

FRANOVA began as a single question: what happens when the world's greatest performance engineering is freed from legacy and given a new identity to grow into? The answer is a collection of six vehicles — each one a familiar silhouette, completely reborn — engineered in-house, badge-swapped in spirit, and delivered under a single promise of craftsmanship.
